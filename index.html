Meet Otter

The Otter edition is a fresh Python and Qt chapter for SnapWord.

Formats: Open and save .docs, .docx, .odt. Import .rtf, .txt, .html, .md. Export PDF and common text formats. Some complex Word and ODT layouts may not convert exactly.
