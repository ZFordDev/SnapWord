# History

SnapWord actually started life as **Lunaris**, an Electron-based rich text editor created on the same day as **SnapDock**.

The two projects were initially developed side by side, but SnapDock ultimately won the popular vote and became the project that moved forward. Lunaris was put on ice.

That wasn't quite the end of it, though.

Several early beta testers continued using Lunaris long after development had stopped, and requests to bring it back kept appearing. The project remained dormant, but it was never completely forgotten.

## From Lunaris to StaxWord

Later, the **StaxDash** organisation needed a rich text editor of its own. Lunaris was taken off the shelf, heavily refactored, and rebuilt as a Python-based application under a new name: **StaxWord**.

By the time StaxDash was wound down, StaxWord had developed into a largely complete application and was approaching beta readiness.

As the StaxDash projects were redistributed, moved into private repositories, or retired, StaxWord was one of the projects considered worth keeping alive.

So it came home.

StaxWord was moved back under **ZFordDev** and reunited with SnapDock as part of the Snap family, becoming **SnapWord**.

## Why SnapWord?

Returning to the original **Lunaris** name has always remained an option.

SnapDock has made the **Snap** name recognisable across the project family, and SnapWord naturally communicates the relationship between the two applications. They're different tools, but they share a common history and philosophy.

At the same time, Lunaris has come up repeatedly as a possible future name. It was where the project began, gives the editor a stronger identity of its own, and remains an important part of its history.

For now, **SnapWord** is the name it carries forward.

## The Ocean Release

SnapWord is planned to reach its first full release as **SnapWord — Otter**, forming part of the wider **Ocean** release generation.

Ocean currently includes:

* **SnapWord — Otter**
* **SnapDock — Guppy (v5)**
* **SnapBoard — Menta**
* **Project: Piranha**

From Lunaris, to StaxWord, to SnapWord, the project has been shelved, revived, rebuilt, renamed, and moved between organisations — but never quite disappeared.

**Otter is where it finally gets its proper release.**
